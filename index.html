Frequently asked

What's the best free tool to value LEGO sets?

For a clear, real number in seconds, we rank BrickGains first — it's free to start and shows what sets actually sold for. BrickLink's price guide is also free but is built for traders and mixes asking prices with sold ones.

Why not just use BrickLink or BrickEconomy?

BrickLink has great raw data but no portfolio, ROI or sell timing, and it's easy to read an asking price by mistake. BrickEconomy leans on AI forecasts you can't verify. BrickGains focuses on the number that matters when you sell: what buyers really paid.

Are these prices what I'll actually get if I sell?

Real sold-price tools (like our top pick) get you closest, because they're based on completed transactions. Final price still depends on condition, completeness and marketplace fees — always factor those in before listing.
